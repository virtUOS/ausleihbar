# Statistics

Lending desk → Analytics → **Statistics** summarizes the loans of your
pools. The statistics only show counts and averages, no names.

![Statistics for 90 days (08/08/2026 – 06/11/2026), all pools: tiles Loans 24, Individual users (period) 5, Users in total 5, Late returns 5 · 23.8% of 21 returned, Ø delay 0.5 days, Currently overdue 1, Ø loan duration 2.5 days · 2.8 h; below them the table "Pools compared" for Media Lab and Podcast Studio and the start of "Inventory & limits"](../bilder/theke-statistik.png)

## Period and pool

At the top you choose **30 days**, **90 days**, **12 months** or **Custom
period** (From/To, at most 5 years) and optionally a **Pool**. Without a
selection, all pools you are responsible for are counted.

## Overview

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Loans** | items handed out in the period |
| **Individual users (period)** | different people who borrowed something in the period |
| **Users in total** | different people who have ever borrowed something from these pools |
| **Late returns** | items returned too late, as a count and as a share of the returned ones |
| **Ø delay** | how late late returns came on average |
| **Currently overdue** | items that are out right now and overdue |
| **Ø loan duration** | separately for daily and hourly products |

"Late returns" only counts what is already back; what is still out appears
under "Currently overdue".

## Pools compared

The table puts the same figures side by side per pool. Every column can be
sorted; the highest value in a column is highlighted, for late returns as a
warning.

## Products

- **Usage over time:** Choose a product to see its bookings as a trend,
  per **Day**, **Week** or **Month** (the day view only for periods of up
  to one year).
- **All products by bookings:** the ranking. Products never borrowed
  appear in it with 0 — candidates for retiring.
- **Rising demand** and **Falling demand:** the products whose bookings
  changed most compared with the period of the same length before.

Here bookings are counted by their start; in the overview above, handed-out
items are counted.

## Defects

**Defective now**, **Ever defective** and **Total incidents**, plus the
**most problematic products** by number of defect incidents. You find the
individual defects under [Defects](items.md#defects).

## Inventory & limits

How many **Items**, **Products** and **Users** there are. If
your installation has set limits for them, it shows how much of each is
used ("…% of limit"); otherwise "no limit".
