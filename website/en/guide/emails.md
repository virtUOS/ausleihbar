# Emails and reminders

Ausleihbar keeps you up to date by email. The emails go to the address
from your sign-in and arrive in the language you chose in the account menu
(German or English). Every email ends with a link to **My bookings**.

!!! info "Texts from your institution"
    Your institution can add its own paragraphs to the emails, such as a
    note about pickup. Pools can also send along a note of their own. The
    details about booking, place and time are always included, though.

## Receipt email

Right after [submitting](cart.md) you get the email **“Ausleihbar reservation
R-… received (awaiting confirmation)”**. It only confirms that the booking
has arrived; it isn't valid yet. It contains:

- the **booking number**,
- per pool a block **Pickup at …** with the items and their period,
  address, opening hours, directions and contact,
- your message to the lending team.

If your cart held items from several pools, the email is called
**“Ausleihbar order received: …”** and lists all booking numbers.
Each pool confirms its part separately.

## Confirmation email

As soon as the lending team has confirmed, you get **“Ausleihbar booking R-…
confirmed — pickup details”**. This is the most important email: keep it
until pickup. It contains:

- the pickup details per pool as in the receipt email,
- possibly **a note from the lending team**, e.g. a suggested pickup
  time,
- as an attachment the **pickup QR code** (`pickup-R-….png`). Show it at
  the desk when you [pick up](pickup-and-return.md); if you scan it
  yourself, it opens your booking's details page,
- as an attachment a **calendar entry** (`booking-R-….ics`) with your
  period, which you can add to your calendar with one click.

!!! note "Partial confirmation for several pools"
    If all pools of your order confirm, you get one combined confirmation
    email. If a pool is still missing, Ausleihbar waits until a fixed time
    of day (set by your institution; 5 pm without a setting of its own) and
    then sends a **partial confirmation**: it says clearly which part is
    already confirmed and which is still pending. If that time has already
    passed or a pickup starts earlier, the email goes out immediately.

For [on-site use](pickup-and-return.md#on-site-use), the email says
**On-site use at …** instead of pickup.

## Reminder the day before pickup

The day before your pickup, **“Ausleihbar booking R-… — pickup tomorrow”**
reminds you of the place, opening hours and directions; for on-site use
it's **“… — use starts tomorrow”**. The email doesn't come at night, but
only from a configurable time of day (9 am without a setting of its own).
Your institution can also switch this reminder off.

## Overdue reminders

If a return is overdue or you missed a pickup, you get
**“Ausleihbar booking R-… — overdue reminder”**. It lists what hasn't been
picked up and what hasn't been returned yet. As long as that stays the
case, the reminder repeats at most about once a day.
The lending team can also remind you from the desk.

## When something changes

Sometimes Ausleihbar has to change a booking. You then get an email:

| Subject (shortened) | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **rescheduled due to a closure** | A block day was entered for your period and your booking was moved. The email gives the old and the new period. |
| **cancelled due to a closure** | Because of a block day the booking couldn't be moved and was cancelled. Contact the lending team for an alternative. |
| **cancelled** | The lending team cancelled your booking. The email lists the items and the period and includes a message, if the team wrote one – such as the reason. |
| **reserved item unavailable** | An item held for you is defective, and there was no replacement. |
| **reserved item may be unavailable** | Someone before you hasn't returned the item yet, and Ausleihbar couldn't move you to another one. Contact the lending team before pickup. Whether and how early this warning comes is set per product. |
| **Strike for your booking …** | The lending team has given you a strike – see [Strikes and blocks](strikes.md). |

If an item is defective and there is an equivalent replacement, Ausleihbar
silently moves you to the replacement. Nothing changes for you then.

!!! tip "Not getting any emails?"
    Check your spam folder. You can always see the status of your bookings
    under [My bookings](my-bookings.md), independently of the emails.
