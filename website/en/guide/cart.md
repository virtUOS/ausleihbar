# Cart and submitting a booking

In the cart you collect everything you want to borrow, even from different
pools and for different periods. Only when you submit the booking does it
go to the lending team.

## The cart

The cart icon in the header shows how many items are in it. Click it to
open the cart.

![Cart with “held until 12:41”, a block for the Media Lab (Room 1.12) with the Sony Alpha 7 IV and a block for the Podcast Studio (Room 0.05) with the Shure SM7B, each from 18/11/2026 to 20/11/2026, below them the optional message field for the lending team and the buttons “Submit booking” and “Discard cart”](../bilder/warenkorb.png)

- The items are grouped by **pool**, and within a pool by **period**. That
  way you see at once where you pick up what.
- With **+** and **−** you add another unit of the same product for the
  same period or remove one, as long as something is free.
- The **trash can** removes a product from the cart.
- **Discard cart** empties the whole cart.

## Held – but not forever

As soon as something is in the cart, a specific item is **held** for you.
Nobody else can book it during that time. The top of the cart shows until
when: “held until 12:41”.

Every action in the cart extends the hold again. How long it lasts is set
by your institution; without a setting of its own it is 30 minutes. If you
don't submit anything in that time, Ausleihbar releases the items again.

!!! warning "Held is not booked yet"
    The cart only holds the items briefly. Only what you have submitted is
    booked – and only what the lending team has confirmed is valid.

## Submitting the booking

1. Check the items, periods and pools.
2. If needed, write a **Message to the lending team**, e.g. “needed for the music
   seminar” or who will pick up the items.
3. Click **Submit booking**.
4. Ausleihbar shows **Booking submitted** with your **Reservation number**
   (e.g. R-00490). From now on you will find the booking under
   [My bookings](my-bookings.md).

!!! note "When the message is required"
    A pool can require you to write a message, for example what you need
    the items for. If an item from such a pool is in the cart, the field is
    called **Message to the lending team (required)**, and the booking can only be
    submitted with a message.

## One booking per pool

If the cart holds items from **several pools**, Ausleihbar turns it into
**one booking per pool** when you submit, each with its own number. The
reason: each pool has its own lending team, and each confirms its part
separately.

You still get only **one** receipt email with all the numbers. Ausleihbar
also combines the confirmations where possible – more on that under
[Emails and reminders](emails.md).

## What happens next

1. You get a **receipt email**: the booking has been received and is
   awaiting confirmation.
2. The lending team checks the booking and **confirms** it. You get the
   **confirmation email** with the pickup QR code and a calendar entry.
3. You pick up the items during the booked period – see
   [Pickup, return and on-site use](pickup-and-return.md).

Until it is confirmed, the booking is shown under My bookings as **Not
confirmed yet — not valid yet**.
