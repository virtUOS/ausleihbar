# My bookings

Under **My bookings** you see all your bookings and their status. You reach
the page through the card on the start page or through the account menu
(your initial at the top right).

![“My bookings”: at the top the “Overdue” section with booking R-00217 (Picked up, “Overdue since 04/11/2026”, Manfrotto 190 tripod), below it “Upcoming” with R-00214 (“Not confirmed yet — not valid yet”, Sony Alpha 7 IV, message “For the media practice seminar”) and the start of R-00219 (“Confirmed”) at the Podcast Studio](../bilder/meine-buchungen.png)

## The sections

- **Overdue** – appears at the very top if there is anything: items you
  should have returned, or a missed pickup. Please return them as soon as
  possible or contact the lending team.
- **Upcoming** – booked for you, but not picked up or used yet.
- **Currently out** – what you have or are using right now and still need
  to return.
- **Returned & cancelled** – completed bookings, nothing left to do.

If you have [strikes](strikes.md), **Your strikes** also appears at the top.

Each booking sits in the card of its **pool**, with room, address and
opening hours. So you know where to go to pick up. Below that are the
booking number, the status, a sentence on what it means for you, the items
with their period and your message to the lending team.

## The statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not confirmed yet — not valid yet** | You have submitted the booking; the lending team still has to confirm it. You can't pick it up yet. |
| **Confirmed** | Confirmed by the lending team. Pick up the items during your period. |
| **Picked up** | You have the items. Return them by the end of the period. |
| **Returned** | Everything is back, all done. |
| **Cancelled** | The booking was cancelled. |

For [on-site use](pickup-and-return.md#on-site-use), two statuses have
different names: **In use** instead of Picked up and **Used** instead of
Returned. The explanations then also talk about using instead of picking up.

Red notices can also appear:

- **Overdue since …** – the return is overdue.
- **Pickup missed (…)** – you didn't pick up the items. Contact the lending
  team.
- **Strike received** – the lending team has issued a strike for this
  booking; the reason is shown in the card.

## A booking in detail

A click on the **booking number** (e.g. R-00215) opens the details page
with status, pool, period and the items with their inventory number.

![Details page of booking R-00215, status “Confirmed” with “Confirmed by the staff. Pick it up during your time slot.”, the Media Lab pool card with address and opening hours, the period 06/11/2026 – 09/11/2026, Zoom H6 with MED-0015 and the “Cancel booking” link](../bilder/buchung-detail.png)

!!! info "QR code and calendar entry"
    You won't find the **pickup QR code** and the **calendar entry**
    (`.ics`) on this page – they are attachments to the
    [confirmation email](emails.md#confirmation-email). When scanned, the QR
    code leads exactly to this details page.

## Cancelling

If something comes up, cancel the booking so that others can use the
items:

- In My bookings, click the booking's **trash can icon**, or
- click **Cancel booking** on its details page.

A confirmation dialog shows you which booking you are cancelling. Confirm
with **Cancel booking** or keep it with **Keep booking**.

!!! warning "Cannot be undone"
    A cancelled booking can't be restored. The items are then free for
    others again. If you need them after all, book again.

You can cancel as long as the booking is **not confirmed yet** or
**confirmed** and nothing from it has been picked up. An
[on-site use without handout](pickup-and-return.md#on-site-use) can only be
cancelled until it starts. After that, the icon is gone. If you can't come
later on, let the lending team know.

!!! tip "Better to cancel than not to show up"
    A booking that is never picked up blocks the item for others. You don't
    automatically get a [strike](strikes.md) for it – that is up to the
    lending team.
